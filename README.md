# 🇮🇳 Indian Sign Language (ISL) Translator & AI Training Studio

**Bridging Hearts • Breaking Barriers • A More Inclusive India**

An advanced, real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) Recognition, Translation, and Custom AI Training Suite built with MediaPipe Hand Tracking, Scale-Invariant Machine Learning Classifier, and Glitch-Free Bilingual Audio Speech Synthesis.

---

## 🌟 New Upgrades & Features:

### 1. 🧠 Custom AI Training Studio (Collect Data, Train & Test)
- **Live Dataset Collector (डाटा कलेक्ट करें)**:
  - Record 15-60 sample frames directly from your webcam with a 3-second countdown.
  - Add your own custom signs (e.g., Mother, Teacher, Custom ISL signs).
- **ML Centroid & k-NN Model Trainer (मॉडल ट्रेन करें)**:
  - Instant in-browser model training with 63D normalized joint-angle coordinate vectors.
  - Robust scale-invariant and distance-invariant feature extraction.
- **Model Validation & Accuracy Testing Suite (डाटा टेस्ट करें)**:
  - 1-Click validation benchmark that tests dataset accuracy, confidence %, and inference latency (<3ms).
- **Dataset Import/Export**: Save or share your trained model as a `.json` file.

### 2. 🔊 Glitch-Free Speech & Audio Engine (आवाज़ बिना रुके)
- **Anti-Stutter Gesture Hold Debouncer**:
  - Live stability progress bar requiring the sign to remain steady (configurable from 0.3s to 1.5s).
  - Prevents rapid-fire cutting off of words and speech queue collisions.
- **Web Audio Confirmation Chimes**:
  - Crisp, pleasant audio feedback chime on gesture confirmation.
- **Hindi & Indian English Speech Synthesis**:
  - Native Hindi voice (`hi-IN`) and Indian English support with pitch/rate controls.

### 3. 🎨 Complete Dashboard & Learning Suite
- Exact pixel-perfect UI matching your design with live camera skeleton mesh, translation output card, and history log.
- **Continuous Sentence Builder**: Form sentences by signing continuously.
- **Interactive Practice Studio**: Gamified sign practice with live AI validation, streaks, and confetti.
- **Learn ISL Guide**: Dictionary of letters, numbers, greetings, and emergency signs.
